1. What conclusion can you reach about the responses to this question?

 -  Students envision themselves fully prepared 5 minutes before the class starts. There are more students that don't have a second monitor, external keyboard, or mouse than students who do have it. Most students plan to takes notes on a computer OR take notes with pen and paper, and computer. Student's questions about this course was about the curiosity of building tech skills, projects, and a data analytic role. Some students had no questions about the course yet.
